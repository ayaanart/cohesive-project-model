## Cohesive Project Model Basic Description
This project model uses *Modes* that you can switch between freely as long as your current *Project State* allows it.
This model makes progress based on what you think should be worked on at the current time,
with the model providing guidance and tools so you do not get lost.

## Modes
Modes are a group of actions that contribute to a single aim.
Every Mode has a unique single aim which is designed to help finish the project.
An individual in only in one mode at a time, but multiple people can be in different modes at the same time.
Every Mode has suggested entering cases and you exit once you complete the actions of that mode, or you choose to leave.

## Jot Mode

### Aim
Stopping *Head Idea*(s) from being forgotten in >30-ish secounds with little effort.

### Possible Use Cases
- *Head Idea*(s) that can not be *Officially Instituted* at the moment for any reason.

### Action(s)
1. Jot your *Head Idea*(s) on your Jotting Pad, which you decided on in Establishing Mode:
The style of your jotting is up to you; you could write, draw or anything else,
but choose a style that is quick, and helps you recall easily.
You do not want a complete neat descriptions, but rather something non-time consuming and is able to help you remember your *Head Idea*.

2. Store your Jotting Pad in the place it belongs, which you decided in the Establishing Mode.

### Edge Cases
- If your Jotting Pad is unavailable:
    - jot in something that you will use/see later.
- If you can not store your Jotting Pad in the correct place:
    - store it in a reasonable place that you will see it later (like on your desk, not in a random drawer)

### Example
You are implementing an enemy for your video game but you are interrupted and need to attend to something else,
but then you just get a brainwave for a new enemy that you have not written anywhere,
so you quickly jot down
"enemy with the same attack that is slow or fast. The slow = parry and fast = dodge. Attack itself same, you watch the speed.",
in your sketchbook and then attend to the thing you have to do.
